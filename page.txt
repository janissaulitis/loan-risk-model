Limits, said plainly

The data is invented. A real version starts with the lender's own history, and the same tests.
It's meant for who to contact early, not for approving or refusing loans. A model that decides on credit for people counts as high-risk under the EU AI Act and needs much more: documentation, human oversight and the right to an explanation.
Risk drifts over time. Check every month: hit rate of the call list, predicted vs actual late rate, results by market. Re-train when they slip.
